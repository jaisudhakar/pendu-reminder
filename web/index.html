How it works

One day with Pendu

Pendu follows your day from the moment you open your laptop to midnight, when the board starts over.

  1. Morning

    1. Open your laptop

    The board shows up on its own. Pendu opens when you log in, and comes back to the front when you wake the laptop from sleep.

  2. Planning

    2. Write today's tasks

    Type a task and press Enter. The note flies up onto the board and gets pinned. Pick a colour, or tick Every day for habits like "Drink water".

  3. All day

    3. Tick things off

    Click a note to check it. It gets crossed out and moves to the end, and the progress bar fills up. Double-click to edit, right-click for more options.

  4. Evening

    4. Nothing gets forgotten

    A task you didn't finish stays on the board the next day, marked from 09/28, until you check it. Clear finished notes with one click.

  5. Midnight

    5. A fresh board every morning

    At midnight the board moves to the new day. Every-day tasks come back unchecked, and yesterday's leftovers are waiting for you.

Features

Small, calm, and always in front of you

💻

Opens at login

The desktop app registers itself to start when you sign in on Windows, macOS or Linux.

🌙

Wakes with your laptop

Pendu keeps running in the background and brings the board forward when you open the lid.

🔁

Every-day tasks

Habits come back unchecked each morning, so you never have to type them again.

📎

Carry-over

Unfinished notes move to the next day with a from date, so nothing slips.

✨

Animated board

Notes sway into place, pins drop in, and notes slide over when they change places.

🔒

Private by default

No account and no server. Notes live on your device, and you can export them to a file.

Get it

Two ways to use Pendu

In your browser

Open the board and start pinning notes. Works on laptops, tablets and phones. Notes are saved in this browser.

On your desktop

The full app, which opens at login and when you wake your laptop. Needs Python 3.8+ with Tkinter.

cd pendu-reminder
