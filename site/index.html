How it works

It shows up. You don't have to go looking.

Most to-do apps wait for you to open them. Pendu opens itself at the moments you're about to start working.

  1. 1

    You log in

    The board opens as soon as you sign in to your computer. Pendu sets this up for you the first time it runs.

  2. 2

    You open the lid

    Pendu keeps running quietly. When your laptop wakes from sleep, the board comes back to the front.

  3. 3001
    3

    A new day starts

    At midnight the board turns over to the new date and shows itself again, ready for today's list.

  4. Pay the billfrom 09/29
    4

    Nothing slips

    A task you didn't finish stays on the board the next day, marked from MM/DD, until you tick it off.

Features

Paper notes, with a few tricks.

Notes fly onto the board

A new note floats up from the input bar, sways like paper and gets pinned with a little pop. Notes slide over when they change places.

Tick things off

Click a note to check it. It fades and gets crossed out, and the progress bar at the top fills up.

Every-day tasks

Tick Every day for things like "Drink water". It comes back unchecked each morning.

Carry-over

Yesterday's unfinished tasks move to today, with the date they were written, so they can't quietly disappear.

Six paper colours

Pick a colour before you stick a note, or change it later from the right-click menu. Double-click to edit.

Yours, offline

Notes live in one small notes.json file on your computer. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

On your desktop

A board that stays out of the way until you need it.

  • Close the window and it just minimises. Pendu keeps watching for wake-up.
  • Only one copy runs at a time. Launching it again brings the board to the front.
  • Switch Open when I log in on or off from the top-right corner.
  • Right-click a note for its colour, repeat-every-day and remove options.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Pendu really free?

Yes. It's free and open source. The code is on GitHub.

Where are my notes stored?

On the desktop, in ~/.pendu/notes.json. Set the PENDU_HOME environment variable, or pass --data FILE, to keep them somewhere else. On the web, they're saved in your browser.

How do I stop it opening at login?

Untick Open when I log in in the top-right corner of the board, or run python -m pendu --remove-autostart.

What happens to tasks I don't finish?

They stay on the board the next day, marked with the date you wrote them, until you tick them off. Finished one-off notes can be cleared with Clear finished.

Can I use the web and desktop versions together?

Yes. Both use the same notes.json format. Export from one and import into the other.
