Most to-do apps wait for you to open them. Pendu opens itself.

It starts when you log in, and comes back to the front every time you wake your laptop. So the first thing you see each morning is the short list of what today is for. That is the whole app.

How it works

  1. 1

    Write it down

    Type a task and press Enter. A paper note floats up, sways into place and gets pinned to the board.

  2. 2

    Open your laptop

    At login, after sleep, and at midnight for a fresh day, the board comes to the front on its own.

  3. 3

    Tick it off

    Click a note to cross it out. Anything you didn't finish carries over to tomorrow, so nothing slips.

Questions

Is Pendu really free?

Yes. No trial, no account, no ads.

Does it need the internet?

No. Pendu works entirely offline and never sends your notes anywhere.

Where are my notes kept?

In ~/.pendu/notes.json on your computer, a plain file you can open, copy or back up. The web version keeps them in your browser.

Why does my computer warn me when I first open it?

Pendu's downloads aren't code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS show a warning the first time. On Windows choose More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click Pendu and choose Open. You only need to do this once. You can also run it from source.

How do I stop it opening at login?

Untick Open when I log in in the top-right corner of the board.

How do I uninstall it?

Untick Open when I log in, quit Pendu, then delete the app. To remove your notes too, delete the .pendu folder in your home folder.

Can I see the code?

Yes, it's all on GitHub. Bug reports and ideas are welcome there.
